# Photorealistic service visual prompts, 2026-06-30

Internal production prompt pack for five CentrLP service visuals.
These prompts are not public page copy.

## Shared direction

1. The visual must look like a photographed working product.
2. The subject is a real business interface on real devices.
3. The scene should be practical, not decorative.
4. Use real desks, glass, metal, paper, cables, notebooks, pens.
5. Show software that appears usable by a business owner or manager.
6. Avoid pastel isometric compositions.
7. Avoid plastic 3D icons.
8. Avoid floating abstract UI cards.
9. Avoid glowing holograms.
10. Avoid sci-fi metaphors.
11. Avoid mascots and robots unless the service directly requires them.
12. Avoid giant logos as the main subject.
13. Avoid fake text walls.
14. Avoid unreadable large Cyrillic.
15. Use only short Russian labels where useful.
16. Make the interface hierarchy understandable even when labels are small.
17. Prefer laptop plus phone, or phone plus tablet, when the service has both customer and manager sides.
18. Keep the crop safe for 1200x630 OpenGraph and the 40:21 service image band.
19. Leave margins around important devices.
20. Keep the main screen sharp enough to read the product purpose.
21. Use natural daylight or soft office light.
22. Include believable screen reflections.
23. Include realistic contact shadows.
