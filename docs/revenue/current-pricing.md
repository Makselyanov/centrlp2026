# CentrLP current pricing notes

Updated: 2026-06-12.

This is an internal operator note for fast replies to incoming project requests.
It is not public site copy.

## Core rule

Do not price a site request only as "layout work" when the client also expects
CMS editing, analytics, launch QA, SEO preparation, advertising readiness, and
post-launch iteration. Quote the first safe scope, then separate support,
promotion, and advertising tests.

## Fast quote matrix

### Static screen or prototype preview

- 1 protected technical preview screen from a mockup: 15,000-25,000 RUB.
- Use when the client needs to see ability before the full estimate.
- Does not include CMS, full adaptive layout, production deployment, analytics,
  SEO, or content management.

### Landing / simple business page

- 5-7 blocks, no CMS or only simple text editing: 45,000-75,000 RUB.
- Add 10,000-20,000 RUB for analytics, events, lead delivery, and basic launch QA
  if not already included.

### Multi-section site from ready desktop/mobile mockups

- Ready visual mockups, 6-8 logical screens/sections, responsive layout,
  messenger links, portfolio/services, admin text editing: 90,000-120,000 RUB.
- Quote 95,000 RUB as the normal anchor for a small local-service site when the
  client provides all design assets and content.
- Raise to 120,000-150,000 RUB when the admin must edit photos, portfolio items,
  menus, service structure, prices, or reusable blocks.

### Site plus sales-readiness contour

- Site, analytics, goals, forms, lead notifications, first advertising-ready
  iteration, and basic conversion QA: from 140,000 RUB.
- Use this when the client says the site must not just exist, but must be
  improved after traffic starts.

## Support and iteration after launch

- Light technical support: from 12,000 RUB/month.
  Includes uptime checks, small text edits, backups, minor fixes, and basic
  consultation.
- Growth support after advertising launch: 20,000-35,000 RUB/month.
  Includes page edits, event checks, conversion observations, small A/B changes,
  and traffic feedback.
- Larger design/development iterations are estimated separately from 15,000 RUB
  per small change or as a monthly sprint.

## Promotion and traffic

- Technical SEO preparation: from 20,000 RUB as a separate task, or included in
  higher site packages as a base layer.
- Monthly SEO for a new local-service site: from 35,000 RUB/month, normally for
  at least 3 months.
- Yandex Direct setup: from 20,000 RUB for search, from 25,000 RUB for RSYA.
- Advertising management: from 30,000 RUB/month.
- Ad budget, copywriting volume, links, photo/video content, and directory
  placements are separate.

  be adjusted based on advertising behavior.

## Message framing

Say:

- "This is not just a static layout; the site needs launch, admin editing,
  analytics, and post-traffic improvements."
- "I can start with a protected first-screen preview so you can see the level."
- "Final scope depends on whether admin editing is only for text or also for
  services, photos, portfolio, prices, and menu structure."
- "Promotion is a separate monthly contour; advertising behavior usually shows
  what needs to be adjusted after launch."

Do not say:

- "I will make the whole site for a small first-screen price."
- "SEO is included forever."
- "A static mockup guarantees conversion."
- "The site will definitely bring leads without traffic and iteration."
